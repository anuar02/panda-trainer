01 · Identity

Personality through purpose.

A new visual asset should make the relationship or task clearer. The product name and final brand mark remain open decisions.

Precise when working

Time, client and action come first. Status color conveys meaning. Records stay consistent across screens.

Personal when joining

A recognizable coach and a clear invitation. Illustration can add warmth at this first encounter.

Calm when progressing

Recorded effort and personal history. Honest gaps in the data, with supportive language.

05 · Before visual expansion

Trust is the first design task.

These findings were reproduced in isolated source-model diagnostics. They are prototype behavior, not claims about a deployed service.

Record continuity

“Saved” must stay saved.

Completion resets result values while announcing success. New-session save announces creation without adding a session.

Meaningful status

A booking isn't a visit.

The client's attendance display derives from scheduled dates. Cancellation, attendance and payment need independent facts.

Measured accessibility

Green text needs adjustment.

Mint ink on its soft surface measures 4.09:1. Small text needs at least 4.5:1; use ink text or a verified darker green.
